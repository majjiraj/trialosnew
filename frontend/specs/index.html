4.2 Eligibility Criteria
4.2.1 Inclusion Criteria
Each subject must meet all of the following criteria to be eligible to participate in this study:
IC-01. Adults ≥ 18 years of age at signing of informed consent.
IC-02. Histologically or cytologically confirmed metastatic or locally advanced NSCLC.
IC-03. Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1 criteria as assessed by investigator.
IC-04. ECOG performance status of 0 or 1 at time of screening.
IC-05. Adequate organ function defined as:
— Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥ 1.5 × 10⁹/L
— Hemoglobin ≥ 9.0 g/dL
— Platelet count ≥ 100 × 10⁹/L
— Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 × ULN
— AST/ALT ≤ 2.5 × ULN (≤ 5 × ULN if liver metastases present)
— Creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault formula)
IC-06. Estimated life expectancy ≥ 12 weeks at the time of randomization.
IC-07. Female subjects of reproductive potential must agree to use highly effective contraception during the study and for 6 months following last dose of investigational product.
4.2.2 Exclusion Criteria
EX-01. Prior systemic therapy for metastatic NSCLC. (Prior adjuvant/neoadjuvant therapy permitted if completed ≥ 12 months before study entry.)
EX-02. Active or untreated CNS metastases.
EX-03. Active autoimmune disease requiring systemic immunosuppression within the past 2 years.
